NPO CRM — Contact Import DND Contacts-Only Correction v1
Date: 2026-09-20

Changed:
- app/Services/ContactService.php

Behavior:
- DND/DNC suppression is checked only against existing Contacts with status=dnc.
- Leads are not checked for DND during Contact import.
- Existing normalized Contact duplicates remain skipped as duplicates.
- Same-file normalized duplicates remain skipped as duplicates.
- DND is counted separately as skipped DND contacts.
- No database/schema changes.

This is a surgical correction to remove the incorrect Lead status='dnd' assumption from the previously deployed import hardening.
