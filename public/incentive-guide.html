How to read the numbers

Net Brokerage = brokerage after configured deductions that affect NB.

Surplus = average monthly NB minus the applicable salary threshold.

Eligible incentive = surplus × applicable slab rate × eligible months.

Provisional release = the configured percentage of eligible incentive released through the incentive ledger as collections are received.

Held reserve = the configured amount retained for true-up.

Actual employee payment is a separate payment event and should not be inferred from a release-ledger row.

Salary history

Salary is effective-dated. Historical months use the salary applicable to those months. Do not overwrite an old salary to change history; add a new effective-dated version with a reason.

Statements

For an incomplete year, read the statement as YTD. A completed annual result should only be treated as final after the year and required data are complete. Quarterly statements show the actual eligible incentive calculation for that quarter.
