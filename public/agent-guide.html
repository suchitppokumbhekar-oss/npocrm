🔑 1. Signing In

1. Open npocrm.propertypoint.online in any browser.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Tap Sign In.

Forgot your password? Tap "Forgot password?" on the login page — you'll get a reset link on your email.

Logging out safely

Tap Logout. The CRM asks you to confirm — tap "OK" to sign out, "Cancel" to stay.

💡 Hitting the browser back button will never log you out by accident. We fixed that.

↑ back to top

📊 2. Your Dashboard

The moment you log in, you see exactly what to do right now:

  • Greeting card — tells you how many overdue tasks you have
  • Quick Actions — New Lead · All Leads · 🔥 Hot Leads · All Tasks · Contacts · Dialer
  • 🚨 OVERDUE — tasks past their time. Do these first.
  • ⏰ DUE SOON — tasks due in the next 2 hours
  • 📅 REST OF TODAY — everything else due before midnight
  • 🏠 Site Visits — visits scheduled for today
  • 📊 Stats & Pipeline — your daily numbers at a glance
  • 📆 Tomorrow — preview of tomorrow's tasks
💡 Golden rule: Work top to bottom. Finish OVERDUE first, then DUE SOON, then REST OF TODAY. Only open other sections when your queue is clear.

↑ back to top

📅 3. Daily Routine

Morning: Log in → look at the greeting → check OVERDUE count → start there.
All day: Every time you call or message a customer, log it immediately — even if they didn't answer.
Evening: Clear your queue → check tomorrow's visits → log out.
💡 Pro tip: Log every call within 5 minutes. Your memory fades; the CRM doesn't.

📞 4. The Task Card UPDATED

Every task card on your dashboard shows three buttons:

📞 💬 ✅ Done
ButtonWhat happens
📞 CallOpens your phone dialer with the customer's number pre-filled.
💬 WhatsAppOpens WhatsApp with the customer's number and a ready-made message.
✅ DoneThis is the important one. Opens the activity modal so you can log what happened.
💡 The ✅ Done button is now bigger and greener — because logging the outcome is the single most important thing you do. Call and WhatsApp are shortcuts for reaching the customer; Done is how you record what happened.

The correct sequence

1. Tap 📞 or 💬 → talk to the customer.
2. Come back to the CRM.
3. Tap ✅ Done → pick the outcome → save.
4. Task disappears. Next action auto-scheduled.

✅ 5. Completing a Task UPDATED

When you open a task by tapping ✅ Done, you'll see:

1. Type — Call / WhatsApp / Email / Meeting / Note / Site Visit (system types hidden)
2. Outcome — required for Call / WhatsApp / Email
3. ⏰ Custom next action time — optional, see §8
4. Notes — what the customer said
5. Save

Searchable outcome dropdown NEW

The Outcome field is now searchable. Tap it → a dropdown appears with outcomes grouped by Positive / Neutral / Negative.

  • Type to filter — e.g. type visit to see all visit-related outcomes
  • Use ↑ ↓ arrows to navigate
  • Press Enter to select
  • Tap the ✕ to clear your selection

You'll only see outcomes relevant to the current activity type and lead stage. If you need an outcome that isn't visible, tap "Show N more outcomes not relevant to this stage".

Logging something outside the task queue

If something happened but there's no task for it (e.g. customer called you directly), scroll to the bottom of the Pending Tasks card on the lead page. There's a small dashed link: "📝 Customer reached out outside the task queue? Log it here".

⚠️ Use the fallback sparingly. Completing a task is how the CRM knows what to schedule next. Free-form logs don't drive the pipeline as cleanly.

🔄 7. Status Advances Automatically

You do not change status manually. You don't need to tick any checkbox. When you pick an outcome, the CRM advances the lead automatically.

💡 When you pick an outcome that has a status mapping, a green line appears: "💡 Status will update to Contacted". That's your confirmation the pipeline will advance. No action needed.

Outcomes that don't change status

These just log what happened and schedule the next attempt — the lead stays where it is:

call_later · not_answered · busy · switched_off · not_reachable · budget_issue · location_issue · visit_interested · visit_needs_family

They still create a followup — the customer doesn't get forgotten — but the pipeline stage stays put.

⚠️ Only pick the outcome that actually happened. If you log "Interested" when the customer wasn't, the CRM will move them to Contacted — and your manager will see the wrong data.

⏰ 8. Custom Follow-up Time

Every outcome has a default next-action delay (e.g. "Interested" → follow-up in 1 hour). But sometimes the customer gives you a specific time.

Example: Customer says "I'm busy now, call me tomorrow at 5 PM."
The default call_later outcome would schedule a retry in 4 hours — not tomorrow 5 PM.
1. Complete Task modal → pick the outcome.
2. Look for the field "⏰ Custom next action time (optional)".
3. Set it to tomorrow 5 PM.
4. Save. The CRM schedules the follow-up for exactly that time.
Customer said…Custom time?
"Call me tomorrow at 5 PM"✅ Set tomorrow 5 PM
"I'm at work, call after 7 PM"✅ Set today 7 PM
"No, I'm busy right now"❌ Leave empty — use default
"Just send me details, I'll reply"❌ Leave empty — use default

🏠 9. Site Visits

Booking the visit

Pick the outcome 🏠 Site visit scheduled on a call. The modal opens a date & time picker — you must fill it in. Set the exact date and time the customer agreed to.

Once saved, the system automatically creates three timed reminders:

  • 🔔 Pre-Visit Confirmation Call — 2 hours before the visit
  • 🔔 Post-Visit Feedback Call — 3 hours after the visit
  • 🔔 Visit Follow-up Call — the day after the visit

Day of the visit

You'll see it under 🏠 Site Visits TODAY on your dashboard.

After the visit

Open the lead → tap the post-visit task → ✅ Done → pick the outcome. The next action schedules itself.

💡 If you pick 🎉 Booked on the spot, the lead auto-advances to Booking. If you pick 👍 Interested, will decide, it stays at Visit Done and schedules a feedback call. If you pick ❌ Not interested, it moves to Lost.

🎉 10. Booking a Deal

When a customer says yes:

1. Complete the task → outcome 🎉 Booking Confirmed.
2. Fill in the booking form:
   • Area (sq ft) + Rate (₹/sq ft) → property value auto-computes
   • Unit / Flat No.
   • Payment Mode (Cheque / NEFT / UPI / Cash)
3. Confirm Brokerage % → amount auto-computes.
4. Save.

The CRM will:

  • ✅ Auto-advance the lead to Booked
  • ⏰ Schedule a Thank You Call for the next day
  • ⏰ Schedule a Brokerage Follow-up in 30 days
  • ❌ Cancel all other pending tasks for that lead
💡 Made a typo? Open the lead → ✏️ Edit Booking to fix it any time.

🏷️ 11. Tags & Labels NEW

Tags and labels let you quickly categorise a lead and find similar leads later. They are independent: one tag + many labels.

Tag — pick one

Every lead can have one tag, showing their buying temperature:

🔥 Hot ☀️ Warm ❄️ Cold 💀 Dead ⭐ VIP

Labels — pick as many as apply

Labels describe the customer's needs and preferences. Grouped into categories:

  • 🏠 Property Type — 1 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK, 4 BHK+, Penthouse, Row House, Villa, Plot, Shop, Office Space, Commercial, Studio
  • 👤 Buyer Type — Investor, Self Use, Resale, Rental
  • 💰 Payment — 100% Down, 50% Down, 20% Down, Regular Payment, Loan Required, Cash Buyer
  • 🏗️ Property Status — Ready to Move-in, Under Construction, New Launch, Possession Soon
  • ⭐ Special — NRI, First Time Buyer, Repeat Customer, Referral

How to tag a lead

1. Open a lead.
2. Look in the header, next to the status badge. Tap 🏷 Add tags.
3. Pick one tag, tick as many labels as apply.
4. Save.

Once saved, the tag chip and labels appear under the customer's name everywhere: leads list, lead detail, search results, dashboard recent leads.

How to find leads by tag or label

Every chip is clickable:

  • Tap 🔥 Hot → see all Hot-tagged leads
  • Tap 🏠 2 BHK → see all 2-BHK-labelled leads

Or use the 🏷️ Quick tag filter at the top of the leads list — one tap to filter.

💡 Tag honestly. If you tag every lead "Hot", the filter becomes useless. Hot means the customer is seriously interested — ready to visit or already visiting.

📇 13. Contacts & Dialer

Access note: Contacts and Dialer are only visible to telecallers and to admins/managers. If you don't see them in the menu, ask your admin to flag you as a telecaller in Settings → Agents.

What's the difference?

  • Leads — people who enquired (through website, ads, walk-in)
  • Contacts — raw numbers from purchased lists that you dial first, and turn into leads only if they're interested

The Dialer — your call queue

1. Tap 📞 Dial Now → phone dialer opens.
2. Talk to the customer.
3. Come back → pick Outcome → enter Duration (in seconds).
4. Add notes → tap 💾 Log & Next →.
5. Next contact loads automatically.
💡 Duration: enter 45 for 45 seconds, 120 for 2 minutes. If you didn't connect, leave it at 0.

Turning a Contact into a Lead

When someone from the contacts list is genuinely interested, open their contact page → tap 🎯 Promote to Lead.

What happens next (automatic):

  • ✅ A new lead is created from the contact
  • ✅ The CRM routes it to the best agent (based on project + team)
  • ✅ A first-contact follow-up is scheduled for that agent in 15 minutes
⚠️ Only promote when they're actually interested. If you promote every dial, your pipeline gets noisy and your manager can't help you prioritise.

⚙️ 16. For Admins ADMIN

Tags & Labels setup

The tag list (Hot / Warm / Cold / Dead / VIP / Watch) and label groups are seeded by default. To edit them, contact a developer — the seed data lives in the database. If you need new tag options, ask.

Contacts & Dialer access

Two ways to control who sees Contacts:

  • Per-agent: Settings → Agents → edit an agent → tick 📞 Telecaller
  • Global: Settings → General → Contacts & Dialer Access → "Telecallers only" or "Everyone"

Admins and team managers always have access regardless of these settings.

Project Routing

Settings → 🎯 Project Routing. For each project you pick:

  • Direct agents — receive leads from this project first
  • Teams — fallback if no direct agent is available

Routing now enforces direct-agent priority properly. If a direct agent is on the project, they get every lead unless they're inactive.

Attendance

The system has attendance tracking ready (office location, check-in radius, agent payroll flag). Activation is pending — talk to your developer if you want to turn it on.

Dev Tools

  • /clear-config?key=DEV_ROUTE_KEY — clears caches
  • /admin/flush-settings?key=DEV_ROUTE_KEY — flushes settings cache
  • /debug-access?key=DEV_ROUTE_KEY — check contacts access for current session
  • /debug-attendance?key=DEV_ROUTE_KEY — check attendance state

🚨 17. Rules to Never Break

Log every call, even if the customer didn't answer.
Never close a task without logging what happened.
Pick honest outcomes — "Interested" when they're not makes the data useless.
Keep notes short but specific — "Asked for 3BHK pricing at Raheja" beats "call done".
Set the visit date accurately — the system creates reminders relative to it.
Tag leads honestly — Hot is for serious buyers, not for anyone who answered the phone.
Use the custom time field if the customer gave you a specific callback time.
Log out only when you mean it — the CRM asks you to confirm every time.

❓ 18. FAQ

I picked an outcome but the status didn't change.

Only some outcomes advance the status. If your outcome doesn't map to a new status (call_later, not_answered), the lead stays where it is — that's correct. See §7.

Where did the "Also update status" checkbox go?

We removed it. The CRM advances the status automatically based on the outcome. You'll see a green info line confirming the change.

Where did the 📅 Custom button on the task card go?

We merged it into the Complete Task flow. Now when you complete a task, the modal has a ⏰ Custom next action time field. Same outcome, cleaner flow.

How do I tag a lead as Hot?

Open the lead → tap the 🏷 Add tags button next to the status badge → pick 🔥 Hot → save. The chip appears next to the customer name everywhere.

I want to see all leads tagged Cold.

Open the leads list → tap the ❄️ Cold chip in the quick filter row. Or tap the Cold chip on any lead — it links to the same filtered view.

Customer said "call me at 5 PM tomorrow". What do I do?

Complete the task → pick the outcome (e.g. call_later) → in the ⏰ Custom next action time field, set tomorrow 5 PM → save.

A customer was lost but called back.

Click 🔄 Revive Lead on the lead page — provide a reason and where to resume.

My Dialer says "Queue is empty".

Either you've called everyone in this filter, or no contacts are assigned to you. Try the "Any callable" filter, or ask your manager to import more contacts.

Where's the "Log Activity" button on the lead page?

We removed it. Complete a task to log an interaction — the Done button is the way. If something genuinely happened outside the task queue, there's a small fallback link at the bottom of the Pending Tasks card.

Can I call directly from the CRM?

Yes — tap 📞 Call on any task or lead card. Your phone dialer opens with the number ready.

खोजने योग्य outcome ड्रॉपडाउन नया
