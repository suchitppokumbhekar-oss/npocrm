🟢 Section A — Agent SOP AGENT

A1 · Daily Start (First 10 Minutes)

  1. Open the CRM. Log in.
  2. Read the greeting card. Note the OVERDUE count.
  3. Open the 🚨 OVERDUE section.
  4. Work through it top to bottom until empty.
  5. Move to ⏰ DUE SOON. Clear it.
  6. Move to 📅 REST OF TODAY.
Do not open email, WhatsApp, or take phone calls before OVERDUE is empty. OVERDUE is the customer you already promised to call back. Breaking this promise costs bookings.

A2 · Handling Any Task (The Only Correct Sequence)

  1. Open the task card.
  2. Tap 📞 Call or 💬 WhatsApp to reach the customer.
  3. Have the conversation.
  4. Return to the CRM.
  5. Tap ✅ Done.
  6. Pick the outcome that actually happened.
  7. Write a note — specific, min 3 words. "Wants 3BHK at Raheja under 1.5Cr" not "call done".
  8. Save.
  9. Verify the task disappeared from the list.
Task doesn't disappear after saving → refresh the page. If it's still there → note the lead ID and tell your manager within 30 minutes.

A3 · Picking an Outcome — Never Break This

Never pick "Interested" if the customer didn't say they're interested.
Never pick an outcome just to clear the task faster.
If you're unsure between two outcomes, pick the more conservative one.
If nothing matches what happened, pick the closest and add the detail in Notes.

Outcomes drive the pipeline. Lying to the CRM means your manager can't help you.

A4 · Site Visit — Full Sequence

  1. Customer agrees to a visit. Outcome: 🏠 Site visit scheduled.
  2. The modal opens a date & time picker. Fill the exact agreed time.
  3. Save. Verify 3 followup tasks appear (pre-visit confirm, post-visit feedback, next day).
  4. On the visit day, complete the pre-visit confirmation call at the scheduled time.
  5. After the visit, complete the post-visit feedback call. Pick the outcome based on what happened.
If the visit is rescheduled, do NOT just edit the visit time. Complete the current pre-visit task with outcome call_later and note the new time. Then log the visit again.

A5 · Booking — Full Sequence

  1. Customer says yes. Complete the task with outcome 🎉 Booking Confirmed.
  2. Fill ALL booking fields:
    • Area (sq ft) — required
    • Rate (₹/sq ft) — required
    • Property Value — auto-computes if area + rate filled
    • Unit / Flat No.
    • Payment Mode (Cheque / NEFT / RTGS / UPI / Cash)
    • Booking Date
  3. Confirm brokerage % (default 2% — change only if the deal is different).
  4. Add co-broker name if any.
  5. Save.
  6. Verify lead status now shows Booking.
  7. Verify a Thank You Call task is scheduled for tomorrow.
Never log a booking without the property value or (area + rate). The brokerage calculation depends on it. Wrong brokerage = wrong commission = dispute.

A6 · Lost Lead — Full Sequence

  1. Complete the task with outcome ❌ Not Interested (or the exact lost outcome).
  2. The modal shows a mandatory Lost Reason field.
  3. Write a specific reason. Format: "Budget mismatch — wanted 60L, property is 75L" NOT "Not interested".
  4. Save.
  5. Verify the lead has moved to Lost status.
Lost leads are re-checked automatically in 90 days. A good reason helps the re-check be useful. A lazy reason makes it waste of time.

A7 · Reviving a Lost Lead

  1. Open the Lost lead.
  2. Tap 🔄 Revive Lead.
  3. Pick the resume status (usually Contacted).
  4. Write the reason (why is this lead back in play?).
  5. Save.
  6. A new first-contact task is scheduled. Complete it like any other task.

A8 · Sharing a Lead

  1. Open the lead. Tap 👥 Assign / Share.
  2. Select the primary agent (if changing).
  3. Tick the additional agents to share with.
  4. Choose the share type:
    • 👥 Internal co-agent — the primary agent gets a reminder in 24h
    • 🏢 On-site sales team — YOU get a reminder in 2 days
  5. Reason note is required. Min 5 words. Explain why the customer needs this agent.
  6. Save.
Never share without a reason. The receiving agent needs context. "Customer interested" is not a reason — it's a fact that was already known.

A9 · Tagging — Do It Within 24 Hours

  1. Open the lead. Tap 🏷 Add tags in the header.
  2. Pick ONE tag:
    • 🔥 Hot — ready to visit or already visited
    • ☀️ Warm — interested, no visit yet
    • ❄️ Cold — not responsive, still trying
    • 💀 Dead — not interested, no hope
    • ⭐ VIP — high-value or referral
    • 👀 Watch — needs monitoring, no action yet
  3. Add at least 2 labels — usually Property Type + Buyer Type.
  4. Save.
Never tag every lead "Hot". Hot means serious buyer. If your Hot list is your whole pipeline, the tag is useless.

A10 · Daily End (Last 10 Minutes)

  1. Clear any remaining OVERDUE — no exceptions.
  2. Check DUE SOON — if any are due after hours, leave them and note the reason.
  3. Check tomorrow's site visits — confirm they're all still on.
  4. Log out.

A11 · What to Do When Something Fails

Task won't disappear after completing → refresh page. Still there → note the lead ID and message manager on WhatsApp within 30 min.
Can't save an outcome (form error) → do NOT retry repeatedly. Send a WhatsApp to manager with: "Couldn't save [customer name] outcome [X]. Error: [text]". Retry once in 5 minutes.
Wrong lead opened by mistake → close it. No harm done. Just don't log anything on the wrong lead.
Customer is angry or abusive → don't log the call. Escalate to your manager immediately. They will handle it.
Customer says something that doesn't fit any outcome → pick the closest outcome, write the exact words in Notes. Manager will clarify later.

🟣 Section M — Team Manager SOP MANAGER

M1 · Morning Check (First 15 Minutes)

  1. Log in. Open your dashboard.
  2. Open 👥 Team Status.
  3. Sort mentally by overdue count.
  4. For each agent with 5+ overdue → tap 📱 Nudge → sends them a WhatsApp with their list.
  5. For each agent with 10+ overdue → call them directly. Not WhatsApp. Call.
  6. For each silent agent (no activity in 24h+) → message them: "Are you working today?"
Do not skip the morning check. Skipping it for one day causes a 3-day backlog.

M2 · Mid-Day Check (Around 12 PM)

  1. Open 📊 Business Pulse → Team Activity Today.
  2. Note agents with 🔴 silent status.
  3. Nudge them again if the morning nudge didn't work.
  4. Open Pipeline Aging.
  5. Note stages with leads 7+ days old. Those leads are dying.
  6. Assign a followup to the relevant agent. Not a task — a WhatsApp message.

M3 · Evening Review (Before You Leave)

  1. Re-open Team Status.
  2. Confirm the morning's overdue count dropped. If it grew → the team isn't executing.
  3. Open 🔥 Hot Leads → filter by agent.
  4. Anyone hoarding Hot leads without action in 24h → flag them.
  5. Open 🏠 Site Visits for tomorrow → confirm each has a pre-visit task scheduled.

M4 · Weekly Review (Monday Morning)

  1. Open /reports/team.
  2. Note last week's conversion per agent.
  3. Note average response times.
  4. Identify top performer and bottom performer.
  5. Top performer → praise in team WhatsApp group.
  6. Bottom performer → 15 min private call. Not a public message.
  7. Any agent below 60% of team average → formal coaching plan.

M5 · Escalation Rules — When to Involve Admin

  1. Agent silent for 2+ consecutive days (no login, no activity) → escalate.
  2. Agent has 10+ overdue for 2+ consecutive days → escalate.
  3. Lead stuck in the same stage for 30+ days → escalate.
  4. Booking disputed by customer → escalate immediately.
  5. Any customer complaint → escalate immediately.
  6. Any error you can't solve within 1 hour → escalate.

🟠 Section X — Admin SOP ADMIN

X1 · Daily Start

  1. Open /reports/crm-health.
  2. Check for: orphaned followups, unassigned leads, stuck statuses.
  3. If orphans exist → run the cleanup SQL (see below).
  4. If unassigned leads exist → check Settings → Project Routing for missing routing.

X2 · New Lead Routing Check

  1. Open Settings → 🎯 Project Routing.
  2. Every active project must have either a direct agent OR a team routed.
  3. Unrouted projects → leads come unassigned → alert fires to admins.
  4. Fix by adding a direct agent or a team.
Check this weekly. Every Monday morning, before the team starts work.

X3 · New Agent Onboarding

  1. Settings → Agents → Add Agent.
  2. Set Name, Email, Phone, Password.
  3. Tick the team(s) this agent belongs to.
  4. Tick 📞 Telecaller only if this agent will work Contacts/Dialer.
  5. Tick 🏢 On Payroll only if this agent needs attendance tracking.
  6. Save.
  7. Give the new agent their login credentials.
  8. Ask them to log in and complete the first test task.

X4 · When to Flush Caches

After ANY raw SQL change to the following tables:

  • lead_statuses
  • lead_status_transitions
  • call_outcomes
  • activity_types
  • followup_action_types
  • lead_sources
  • lead_tags, lead_labels
  • settings
  1. Visit /admin/flush-settings?key=DEV_ROUTE_KEY → should say "✅ Settings cache flushed."
  2. Visit /clear-config?key=DEV_ROUTE_KEY → should say "✅ Config + Route + View + App caches cleared."
Never edit statuses/outcomes/activity types via SQL and forget the flush. Users will see "Invalid transition" errors and won't understand why.

X5 · Weekly Data Hygiene

  1. Run this SQL and verify the count is 0: 
    SELECT COUNT(*) AS orphaned
FROM followups f
JOIN leads l ON l.id = f.lead_id
WHERE f.status = 'pending'
  AND l.agent_id IS NOT NULL
  AND f.agent_id != l.agent_id
  AND NOT EXISTS (
    SELECT 1 FROM lead_agents la
    WHERE la.lead_id = f.lead_id
      AND la.agent_id = f.agent_id
      AND la.is_active = 1
  );
  2. Check for unassigned active leads: 
    SELECT COUNT(*) FROM leads
WHERE agent_id IS NULL
  AND status NOT IN ('booking', 'lost');
  3. If any count is above 0 → investigate and fix before Monday.

X6 · Emergency Procedures

CRM shows 500 error on every page → clear caches first (/clear-config). Still broken → check storage/logs/laravel.log. If it's a code error → revert the last deployment.
Data loss (deleted leads or missing records) → restore from last backup. Never try to "fix" missing data by re-entering manually. Restore then merge.
Security breach (suspicious logins, data scraping) → rotate DEV_ROUTE_KEY in .env. Force-logout all users by clearing the sessions table. Reset all agent passwords.
Booking dispute from customer → pull the lead's timeline from CRM. Screenshot all activities. Send to manager + customer within 24h.

🟢 सेक्शन A — एजेंट SOP AGENT

A1 · दिन की शुरुआत (पहले 10 मिनट)

  1. CRM खोलें। लॉगिन करें।
  2. Greeting card पढ़ें। OVERDUE count देखें।
  3. 🚨 OVERDUE सेक्शन खोलें।
  4. ऊपर से नीचे तक सब करें — खाली होने तक।
  5. ⏰ DUE SOON पर जाएँ। साफ करें।
  6. फिर 📅 REST OF TODAY।
OVERDUE खाली करने से पहले email, WhatsApp, या फ़ोन कॉल न उठाएँ। OVERDUE वह ग्राहक है जिसे आपने कॉलबैक का वादा किया था।

A2 · कोई भी टास्क हैंडल करना

  1. टास्क कार्ड खोलें।
  2. 📞 Call या 💬 WhatsApp टैप करें।
  3. ग्राहक से बात करें।
  4. CRM पर वापस आएं।
  5. ✅ Done टैप करें।
  6. जो सच में हुआ वह outcome चुनें।
  7. नोट लिखें — कम से कम 3 शब्द, विशिष्ट।
  8. सेव करें।
  9. टास्क गायब हुआ verify करें।
टास्क सेव के बाद गायब नहीं हुआ → पेज refresh करें। अगर फिर भी है → lead ID नोट करें और 30 मिनट में manager को बताएं।

A3 · Outcome चुनना — यह कभी न तोड़ें

अगर ग्राहक ने interested नहीं कहा, तो "Interested" कभी न चुनें।
टास्क जल्दी हटाने के लिए कोई outcome न चुनें।
दो outcome में उलझन हो → ज़्यादा conservative वाला चुनें।
कुछ भी match न हो → सबसे करीब वाला चुनें + Notes में असली बात लिखें।

A4 · साइट विजिट — पूरा क्रम

  1. ग्राहक विजिट के लिए राज़ी → outcome 🏠 Site visit scheduled।
  2. Modal तारीख़-समय पिकर खोलेगा। ग्राहक ने जो सही समय बताया वही भरें।
  3. सेव करें। 3 followup tasks आने verify करें।
  4. विजिट के दिन pre-visit confirmation call करें।
  5. विजिट के बाद post-visit feedback call करें। Outcome चुनें।
विजिट reschedule हो → सिर्फ़ visit time edit न करें। वर्तमान task को call_later outcome के साथ पूरा करें, नया समय Notes में लिखें। फिर दोबारा visit log करें।

A5 · बुकिंग — पूरा क्रम

  1. ग्राहक ने हाँ कहा → outcome 🎉 Booking Confirmed।
  2. बुकिंग के सभी fields भरें: Area, Rate, Property Value, Unit, Payment Mode, Booking Date।
  3. Brokerage % confirm करें (default 2%)।
  4. Co-broker नाम अगर हो।
  5. सेव करें।
  6. Lead status Booking हुआ verify करें।
  7. Thank You Call कल के लिए तय हुआ verify करें।
Property value या (Area + Rate) के बिना कभी booking log न करें। Brokerage इसी पर निर्भर है।

A6 · Lost Lead

  1. Outcome ❌ Not Interested (या सही lost outcome) चुनें।
  2. Lost Reason field ज़रूरी है।
  3. विशिष्ट कारण लिखें: "Budget mismatch — wanted 60L, property 75L" न कि "Not interested"।
  4. सेव करें।
  5. Lead Lost हुआ verify करें।

A7 · Lost Lead Revive करना

  1. Lost lead खोलें।
  2. 🔄 Revive Lead टैप करें।
  3. Resume status चुनें (आमतौर पर Contacted)।
  4. कारण लिखें (क्यों यह lead वापस आ रही है?)।
  5. सेव करें।
  6. नया first-contact task तय होगा। उसे normal task की तरह पूरा करें।

A8 · Lead शेयर करना

  1. Lead खोलें। 👥 Assign / Share टैप करें।
  2. Primary agent चुनें (अगर बदलना है)।
  3. अतिरिक्त agents को tick करें।
  4. Share type चुनें:
    • 👥 Internal co-agent — primary को 24h में reminder
    • 🏢 On-site sales team — आपको 2 दिन में reminder
  5. कारण लिखें — कम से कम 5 शब्द।
  6. सेव करें।
बिना कारण कभी share न करें। Receiving agent को context चाहिए।

A9 · टैग करना — 24 घंटे के अंदर

  1. Lead खोलें। 🏷 Add tags टैप करें।
  2. एक tag चुनें:
    • 🔥 Hot — विजिट के लिए तैयार या विजिट हो चुका
    • ☀️ Warm — interested, विजिट नहीं हुआ
    • ❄️ Cold — जवाब नहीं, कोशिश जारी
    • 💀 Dead — interested नहीं, उम्मीद नहीं
    • ⭐ VIP — high-value या referral
    • 👀 Watch — नज़र रखनी है, action नहीं
  3. कम से कम 2 labels जोड़ें — Property Type + Buyer Type।
  4. सेव करें।
हर lead को "Hot" कभी न करें। Hot का मतलब serious buyer है।

A10 · दिन का अंत (आख़िरी 10 मिनट)

  1. बाकी OVERDUE साफ करें — कोई अपवाद नहीं।
  2. DUE SOON देखें।
  3. कल के site visits confirm करें।
  4. लॉगआउट करें।

A11 · कुछ फेल हो जाए तो

टास्क नहीं हटा → refresh। फिर भी → 30 मिनट में manager को WhatsApp करें।
Outcome सेव नहीं हुआ → बार-बार कोशिश न करें। WhatsApp पर manager को बताएं।
गलत lead खोल लिया → बंद करें। कोई नुकसान नहीं।
ग्राहक गुस्से में → कॉल लॉग न करें। तुरंत manager को बताएं।
ग्राहक ने कुछ ऐसा कहा जो किसी outcome में नहीं आता → सबसे करीब वाला चुनें + Notes में असली बात लिखें।

🟣 सेक्शन M — टीम मैनेजर SOP MANAGER

M1 · सुबह की जाँच (पहले 15 मिनट)

  1. लॉगिन करें। Dashboard खोलें।
  2. 👥 Team Status खोलें।
  3. Overdue count के हिसाब से देखें।
  4. 5+ overdue वाले agents → 📱 Nudge टैप करें।
  5. 10+ overdue वाले → सीधे कॉल करें। WhatsApp नहीं, कॉल।
  6. Silent agents (24h से कोई activity नहीं) → message: "आज काम कर रहे हो?"
सुबह की जाँच कभी skip न करें। एक दिन skip = 3 दिन का backlog।

M2 · दोपहर की जाँच (12 बजे)

  1. 📊 Business Pulse → Team Activity खोलें।
  2. 🔴 silent agents नोट करें।
  3. फिर से Nudge करें।
  4. Pipeline Aging खोलें।
  5. 7+ दिन पुराने leads वाले stages नोट करें।
  6. Relevant agent को WhatsApp पर followup दें।

M3 · शाम की समीक्षा (जाने से पहले)

  1. फिर से Team Status देखें।
  2. सुबह का overdue घटा या बढ़ा — check करें।
  3. 🔥 Hot Leads खोलें → agent filter।
  4. Hot leads पर 24h action नहीं → flag करें।
  5. कल के Site Visits confirm करें।

M4 · साप्ताहिक समीक्षा (सोमवार सुबह)

  1. /reports/team खोलें।
  2. पिछले हफ़्ते की conversion देखें।
  3. Response times देखें।
  4. Top + bottom performer पहचानें।
  5. Top performer की तारीफ़ team group में करें।
  6. Bottom performer से 15 मिनट private बात करें।
  7. Team average से 60% नीचे वाले agent के लिए formal coaching plan।

M5 · Escalation — कब Admin को बताएं

  1. Agent 2+ दिन silent (लॉगिन नहीं, activity नहीं) → escalate।
  2. Agent 10+ overdue 2+ दिन → escalate।
  3. Lead 30+ दिन एक stage में → escalate।
  4. Booking disputed → तुरंत escalate।
  5. ग्राहक complaint → तुरंत escalate।
  6. 1 घंटे में solve न हुआ error → escalate।

  1. /admin/flush-settings?key=DEV_ROUTE_KEY → "✅ Settings cache flushed."
  2. /clear-config?key=DEV_ROUTE_KEY → "✅ Config + Route + View + App caches cleared."
Raw SQL edit के बाद flush भूल गए → "Invalid transition" errors आएँगे।

X5 · साप्ताहिक Data Hygiene

  1. Orphaned followups count करें — 0 होना चाहिए।
  2. Unassigned active leads check करें — 0 होना चाहिए।
  3. 0 से ऊपर → सोमवार से पहले ठीक करें।

X6 · आपातकालीन प्रक्रिया

हर पेज पर 500 error → पहले cache clear करें। फिर भी → storage/logs/laravel.log देखें। Code error → पिछली deployment revert करें।
Data loss → backup से restore करें। Manual entry न करें।
Security breach → DEV_ROUTE_KEY rotate करें। Sessions table clear करें। सभी agent passwords reset करें।
Booking dispute → lead timeline का screenshot लें। 24h में manager + customer को भेजें।

🟢 विभाग A — एजंट SOP AGENT

A1 · दिवसाची सुरुवात (पहिले 10 मिनिटे)

  1. CRM उघडा. लॉगिन करा.
  2. Greeting card वाचा. OVERDUE count पहा.
  3. 🚨 OVERDUE विभाग उघडा.
  4. वरून खाली सर्व करा — रिकामे होईपर्यंत.
  5. ⏰ DUE SOON वर जा. संपवा.
  6. नंतर 📅 REST OF TODAY.
OVERDUE रिकामे करण्यापूर्वी email, WhatsApp, किंवा फोन कॉल उचलू नका. OVERDUE म्हणजे ज्या ग्राहकाला तुम्ही कॉलबॅकचे वचन दिले आहे.

A2 · कोणताही टास्क हाताळणे

  1. टास्क कार्ड उघडा.
  2. 📞 Call किंवा 💬 WhatsApp टॅप करा.
  3. ग्राहकाशी बोला.
  4. CRM वर परत या.
  5. ✅ Done टॅप करा.
  6. खरोखर घडलेला outcome निवडा.
  7. नोट लिहा — किमान 3 शब्द, विशिष्ट.
  8. सेव्ह करा.
  9. टास्क गेला verify करा.
टास्क सेव्ह केल्यावर गेला नाही → पान refresh करा. तरीही → lead ID नोंदवा आणि 30 मिनिटांत manager ला सांगा.

A3 · Outcome निवडणे — हे कधीही मोडू नका

ग्राहकाने interested म्हटले नसेल तर "Interested" कधीही निवडू नका.
टास्क लवकर हटवण्यासाठी outcome निवडू नका.
दोन outcome मध्ये गोंधळ → जास्त conservative निवडा.
काहीही match नाही → जवळचा निवडा + Notes मध्ये खरे लिहा.

A4 · साइट भेट — पूर्ण क्रम

  1. ग्राहक भेटीला तयार → outcome 🏠 Site visit scheduled.
  2. Modal तारीख-वेळ picker उघडेल. ग्राहकाने दिलेली तंतोतंत वेळ भरा.
  3. सेव्ह करा. 3 followup tasks आले verify करा.
  4. भेटीच्या दिवशी pre-visit confirmation call करा.
  5. भेटीनंतर post-visit feedback call करा. Outcome निवडा.
भेट reschedule झाली → फक्त visit time edit करू नका. सध्याचा task call_later outcome ने संपवा, नवीन वेळ Notes मध्ये लिहा. नंतर पुन्हा visit log करा.

A5 · बुकिंग — पूर्ण क्रम

  1. ग्राहक हो म्हणाला → outcome 🎉 Booking Confirmed.
  2. सर्व booking fields भरा: Area, Rate, Property Value, Unit, Payment Mode, Booking Date.
  3. Brokerage % confirm करा (default 2%).
  4. Co-broker नाव असल्यास.
  5. सेव्ह करा.
  6. Lead status Booking verify करा.
  7. Thank You Call उद्यासाठी ठरले verify करा.
Property value किंवा (Area + Rate) शिवाय booking कधीही log करू नका. Brokerage यावर अवलंबून आहे.

A6 · Lost Lead

  1. Outcome ❌ Not Interested (किंवा योग्य lost outcome).
  2. Lost Reason field आवश्यक.
  3. विशिष्ट कारण: "Budget mismatch — wanted 60L, property 75L" नाही "Not interested".
  4. सेव्ह करा.
  5. Lead Lost verify करा.

A7 · Lost Lead Revive

  1. Lost lead उघडा.
  2. 🔄 Revive Lead टॅप करा.
  3. Resume status निवडा (सामान्यतः Contacted).
  4. कारण लिहा.
  5. सेव्ह करा.
  6. नवीन first-contact task ठरेल. तो सामान्य task सारखा पूर्ण करा.

A8 · Lead शेअर करणे

  1. Lead उघडा. 👥 Assign / Share.
  2. Primary agent निवडा (बदलायचे असल्यास).
  3. अतिरिक्त agents tick करा.
  4. Share type:
    • 👥 Internal co-agent — primary ला 24h मध्ये reminder
    • 🏢 On-site sales team — तुम्हाला 2 दिवसांत reminder
  5. कारण लिहा — किमान 5 शब्द.
  6. सेव्ह करा.
कारणाशिवाय कधीही share करू नका.

A9 · टॅग करणे — 24 तासांत

  1. Lead उघडा. 🏷 Add tags.
  2. एक tag:
    • 🔥 Hot — भेटीला तयार किंवा भेट झाली
    • ☀️ Warm — interested, भेट नाही
    • ❄️ Cold — प्रतिसाद नाही
    • 💀 Dead — interested नाही
    • ⭐ VIP — high-value किंवा referral
    • 👀 Watch — लक्ष ठेवायचे
  3. किमान 2 labels — Property Type + Buyer Type.
  4. सेव्ह करा.
प्रत्येक lead "Hot" करू नका. Hot म्हणजे serious buyer.

A10 · दिवसाचा शेवट (शेवटचे 10 मिनिटे)

  1. उरलेले OVERDUE संपवा — अपवाद नाही.
  2. DUE SOON पहा.
  3. उद्याच्या site visits confirm करा.
  4. लॉगआउट करा.

A11 · काही fail झाले तर

टास्क गेला नाही → refresh. तरीही → 30 मिनिटांत manager ला WhatsApp.
Outcome सेव्ह झाला नाही → वारंवार प्रयत्न नका. WhatsApp वर manager ला कळवा.
चुकीचे lead उघडले → बंद करा. काही नुकसान नाही.
ग्राहक रागावला → कॉल log करू नका. तात्काळ manager ला कळवा.
ग्राहकाने काहीतरी सांगितले जे कोणत्याही outcome मध्ये नाही → जवळचा निवडा + Notes मध्ये खरे लिहा.
